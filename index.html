Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Ctrl+O: Open one or more media files.
  • Ctrl+Shift+O: Open Project.
  • Ctrl+S: Save Project.
  • Ctrl+Shift+S: Save Project As.
  • Ctrl+Page Up or Ctrl+Page Down: Move to the previous or next editor section.
  • Ctrl+Up: Move Item Earlier.
  • Ctrl+Down: Move Item Later.
  • Alt+Enter: Item Properties.
  • Delete: Remove Item.
  • Ctrl+Tab or Ctrl+Shift+Tab: Switch between Media Editor and AudioStudio Pro.
  • X: Play or pause from the current playhead.
  • Space: Audition around the current playhead and return to it.
  • Escape: Close a dialog or cancel a temporary operation.
  • B, V, or A: Target both tracks, video only, or audio only.
  • U or I: Silently move backward or forward 1 second and play a short media sample. JAWS does not announce each scrub.
  • Shift+U or Shift+I: Scrub backward or forward 100 milliseconds.
  • Ctrl+Shift+U or Ctrl+Shift+I: Scrub backward or forward 10 milliseconds.
  • Left bracket or Right bracket: Set the In or Out mark.
  • Left or Right Arrow: Move backward or forward 5 seconds.
  • Shift+Left or Shift+Right: Move backward or forward 30 seconds.
  • J or L: Move backward or forward 5 minutes.
  • Alt+Left or Alt+Right: Move by 1 millisecond.
  • Home or End: Move to the beginning or end.
  • Ctrl+G: Enter an exact playhead time using Hours, Minutes, Seconds, and Milliseconds.
  • Ctrl+K: Split the selected item at the playhead without changing the source file.

Testing Guide

Workspaces: Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab switch between Media Editor and AudioStudio Pro. AudioStudio Pro is intentionally a placeholder in this build.

Moving through Media Editor: With the JAWS Virtual Cursor off, press Ctrl+Page Up or Ctrl+Page Down to move among Media Library, every named media editor, and Output. Tab moves within the active section.

Primary and Source Media: The first opened file becomes Primary Media. Additional files enter Source Media and are excluded from output until inserted.

Dedicated media editors: Every playable Primary and Source item has its own titled editor section, preview player, playhead slider, position, marks, and controls.

Activating media: Arrow to an item in Primary Sequence or Source Media to activate it, or press Enter to move directly into its editor. Opening another source does not take control away from the Primary editor.

Playback: X plays or pauses continuously. Space auditions around the playhead and returns to the original position.

Scrubbing: U and I scrub by one second. Shift+U and Shift+I scrub by 100 milliseconds. Ctrl+Shift+U and Ctrl+Shift+I scrub by 10 milliseconds. Scrubbing intentionally produces no JAWS status announcement so you can hear the media sample.

Track targeting: B targets both audio and video. V targets video only. A targets audio only. Audio files always target audio.

Setting edit points: Left bracket sets the In mark. Right bracket sets the Out mark. Ctrl+G opens exact-time fields.

Building the sequence: Mark a selection in Source Media, then use Insert Selection at Primary Playhead or Add Selection to End.

Returning to reading: Press Insert+Z to turn the Virtual Cursor on again.

Native Open and Save dialogs: Virtual Cursor settings do not apply inside Windows file dialogs.

This build establishes nondestructive Primary and Source Media architecture. It does not yet render or export the complete sequence.
