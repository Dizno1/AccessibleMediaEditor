Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Ctrl+O: Open one or more media files.
  • Ctrl+Shift+O: Open Project.
  • Ctrl+S: Save Project.
  • Ctrl+Shift+S: Save Project As.
  • Ctrl+Page Up or Ctrl+Page Down: Move to the previous or next editor section.
  • Ctrl+Up: Move Item Earlier.
  • Ctrl+Down: Move Item Later.
  • Alt+Enter: Item Properties.
  • Delete: Remove Item.
  • Ctrl+P: Preview from Selected Item.
  • Escape: Stop or close a dialog.
  • U or I: Scrub backward or forward 1 second.
  • Shift+U or Shift+I: Scrub backward or forward 100 milliseconds.
  • Ctrl+Shift+U or Ctrl+Shift+I: Scrub backward or forward 10 milliseconds.
  • Left bracket or Right bracket: Set the In or Out mark.
  • Left or Right Arrow: Move backward or forward 5 seconds.
  • Shift+Left or Shift+Right: Move backward or forward 30 seconds.
  • J or L: Move backward or forward 5 minutes.
  • Alt+Left or Alt+Right: Move by 1 millisecond.
  • Home or End: Move to the beginning or end.
  • Ctrl+G: Enter an exact playhead time using Hours, Minutes, Seconds, and Milliseconds.
  • Ctrl+K: Split the selected item at the playhead without changing the source file.

Testing Guide

Moving through the editor: With the JAWS Virtual Cursor off, press Ctrl+Page Up or Ctrl+Page Down to move among Media, Playback, Edit, and Output. Then use Tab within that section.

Selecting media: With the Virtual Cursor on, navigate to Open media and position JAWS inside the list box. Then press Insert+Z to turn the Virtual Cursor off. Use plain Up Arrow and Down Arrow to select different items.

Rearranging media: With focus inside Open media and the Virtual Cursor off, use Ctrl+Up Arrow and Ctrl+Down Arrow. These move the selected item; they do not select another item.

Using the playhead: With the Virtual Cursor off, use U and I to scrub by one second. Shift+U and Shift+I scrub by 100 milliseconds. Ctrl+Shift+U and Ctrl+Shift+I scrub by 10 milliseconds. Left and Right Arrow move by 5 seconds. Shift+Left and Shift+Right move by 30 seconds. Alt+Left and Alt+Right move by one millisecond.

Setting edit points: With the Virtual Cursor off, press left bracket for the In mark or right bracket for the Out mark. Ctrl+G opens human-readable exact-time fields. Ctrl+K splits at the playhead.

Returning to reading: Press Insert+Z to turn the Virtual Cursor on again.

Native Open and Save dialogs: Virtual Cursor settings do not apply inside Windows file dialogs.
