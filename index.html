    Instructions and Keyboard Shortcuts

    Screen reader setup

    Accessible Production Studio uses application keyboard commands. The screen reader must pass X, the Arrow keys, and the other editing commands to the application.

    NVDA: Press NVDA+Space to enter Focus Mode. Press NVDA+Space again when you want to return to Browse Mode.

    JAWS: Press Insert+Z to turn the Virtual PC Cursor off. Press Insert+Z again when you want to turn it back on.

    Narrator: Press Narrator+Space to turn Scan Mode off. Press Narrator+Space again when you want to turn it back on.

    Use the application interaction mode while working in Media Editor, Presentation, AudioStudio Pro, media lists, and production sequences. These instructions are available from every workspace with F1.

    Choose a workspace

    Media Editor assembles and edits video, audio, images, and text in Primary Production. Presentation builds an independent ordered presentation with title slides, transitions, narration, background music, ducking, preview, project saving, and MP4 export. AudioStudio Pro records audio, organizes ordinary audio and audiobooks, edits selected sources, changes playback speed, shortens long pauses nondestructively, and exports audio copies.

    Press Ctrl+Tab or Ctrl+Shift+Tab to move among the three workspaces. Press Ctrl+Page Up or Ctrl+Page Down to move among sections inside the active workspace.

    Opening work

    Ctrl+O opens ordinary files and recognizes an AMEPROJECT automatically. Ctrl+Shift+O opens a choice between an Accessible Production Studio project and an audiobook folder. Either structured item can be opened from any workspace, and the application moves to the correct workspace.

    Opening, editing, shortening pauses, and arranging presentation items are nondestructive. Original files are not deliberately changed or deleted. Save an AMEPROJECT when you need to reopen an editable production or presentation. Export creates a finished media file.

    App-wide commands

    • Ctrl+O: Open ordinary files for the active workspace. In AudioStudio Pro, this opens one or more audio files.
    • Ctrl+Shift+O: Open a choice between an editable AMEPROJECT and an audiobook or audio folder. Either choice works from every workspace and moves to the correct workspace.
    • Ctrl+S: Save Project.
    • Ctrl+Shift+S: Save Project As.
    • Ctrl+E: Render and export the complete Primary Production.
    • Ctrl+Page Up or Ctrl+Page Down: Move to the previous or next editor section.
    • Ctrl+Up: Move Item Earlier.
    • Ctrl+Down: Move Item Later.
    • Alt+Enter: Item Properties.
    • Delete: Remove Item.
    • Ctrl+Tab or Ctrl+Shift+Tab: Switch between Media Editor, Presentation, and AudioStudio Pro.
    • F1: Open these instructions and keyboard shortcuts from any workspace.
    • X: Play or pause the active media or production from the current playhead.
    • Space: Audition the complete bracket selection, or play continuously from the current playhead when no brackets are set. Press Space again to stop and return.
    • Escape: Close a dialog or cancel a temporary operation.
    • In Primary Production, B, V, or A targets both tracks, video only, or audio only for replacement of a selected range.
    • U or I: Silently move backward or forward 1 second and play a short media sample. JAWS does not announce each scrub.
    • Shift+U or Shift+I: Scrub backward or forward 100 milliseconds.
    • Ctrl+Alt+U or Ctrl+Alt+I: Scrub backward or forward 10 milliseconds.
    • Left bracket or Right bracket: Set the matching selection boundary.
    • Left or Right Arrow: Move backward or forward 5 seconds. During playback, continue playing from the new position.
    • Shift+Left or Shift+Right: Move backward or forward 30 seconds.
    • J or L: Move backward or forward 5 minutes.
    • Alt+Left or Alt+Right: Move by 1 millisecond.
    • Home or End: Move to the beginning or end.
    • Ctrl+G: Enter an exact playhead time using Hours, Minutes, Seconds, and Milliseconds.
    • Ctrl+C: Copy the marked selection from the active Source editor.
    • Ctrl+V: Insert at the playhead or empty brackets. When Primary contains a non-empty selected range, replace the track chosen with A, V, or B.
    • Ctrl+Shift+V: Explicitly insert copied media into the sequence and increase production duration.
    • Ctrl+K: Split the selected item at the playhead without changing the source file.
    • Ctrl+Delete: Apply the bracketed edit in Primary Production or the active Source editor.

    Presentation commands

    • Start New Presentation Sequence clears only the presentation sequence. It does not save a file.
    • Open Presentation Project opens an existing AMEPROJECT file directly. Ctrl+Shift+O opens the project-or-audiobook choice from any workspace.
    • Save Presentation Project or Ctrl+S saves an AMEPROJECT containing the sequence, source references, durations, descriptions, and music settings.
    • Save Presentation Project As or Ctrl+Shift+S chooses a new AMEPROJECT filename and location.
    • Export creates the finished MP4. The MP4 can be edited as one video, but its separate presentation components cannot be recovered from the export.
    • In the Production items list, Ctrl+Space selects or deselects one item.
    • Shift+Arrow extends a selection, and Ctrl+A selects every presentation item.
    • The Presentation Timeline is the active editing area and appears before the Source Media Library.
    • Alt+P moves to the Presentation Timeline. Alt+S moves to the source library in the active workspace.
    • Ctrl+X cuts the current or selected presentation items. Ctrl+C copies them. Ctrl+V pastes them before the current timeline item while preserving their order.
    • Delete removes selected sequence items without deleting shared library media.
    • Preview Complete Presentation renders an accurate preview before export. In the preview, X plays or pauses and Left or Right Arrow moves five seconds.
    • Background music automatically lowers during video or audio items. The music controls set both its normal level and its reduced level.

    AudioStudio Pro commands

    • Enable Microphone Access makes Windows request microphone permission and lists the available microphones.
    • Ctrl+Alt+R starts or stops recording while AudioStudio Pro is active.
    • Playback speed changes listening speed without changing the saved source duration or pitch. Press Comma to decrease speed or Period to increase speed in Media Editor or Audio Editor.
    • Analyze and Create Shortened Copies processes one or more selected chapters, reports the proposed reduction, asks for confirmation, and saves the results in a separate Shortened Pauses collection for that book. The originals are never changed.
    • Export Selected Audio Copies to Folder creates ordinary audio files outside the app.
    • Ctrl+Alt+Space pauses or resumes an active recording.
    • After stopping, review the recording before keeping it, recording again, or discarding it.
    • Kept recordings enter the Shared Audio Library and can be deliberately added to Media Editor or Presentation.
    • Open Audio Files imports selected files. Open Audiobook or Audio Folder creates or updates a collection named from the chosen folder and opens that collection in natural chapter-number order.
    • The Audio collection selector separates All Audio, Other Audio, Recordings, each imported book, and each book's Shortened Pauses copies.
    • Use Ctrl+Space or Shift+Arrow in Shared Audio Library to select multiple chapters. Each change announces the filename, selected or unselected state, and total selected. Ctrl+A selects every audio file in the current collection.
    • Remove Selected Audio from Shared Library removes library entries without deleting original chapter files.
    • Alt+S moves directly to Shared Audio Library while AudioStudio Pro is active. In Media Editor, Alt+S moves to Shared Media Library when the current project has no sources.
    • Preview Selected Audio is a toggle. Activate it again, or press X, to stop the library preview.
    • Ctrl+Page Up or Ctrl+Page Down moves among Recording, Recording Review when available, Audio Source Library, and Audio Editor.
    • Open Selected Audio in Audio Editor activates the selected source without changing the original file.
    • In Audio Editor, X plays or pauses. Arrow keys, U and I, Home and End, Alt+Arrow, and Ctrl+G use the same timing model as Media Editor.
    • Left and Right Brackets select a range. Ctrl+Delete or Keep Bracketed Range as New Audio Source saves that range as a new nondestructive source.

    Open Project or Audiobook

    Choose what you want to open. Open Project is the first choice from every workspace. The application will move to the correct workspace.

    Testing Guide

    Workspaces: Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab switch between Media Editor, Presentation, and AudioStudio Pro. AudioStudio Pro supports microphone recording, review, and the shared Source Media Library in this build.

    Moving through Media Editor: With the JAWS Virtual Cursor off, press Ctrl+Page Up or Ctrl+Page Down to move among Media Library, every named media editor, and Output. Tab moves within the active section.

    Primary and Source Media: The first file returned by the Windows dialog becomes Primary Media. Additional files enter Source Media and are excluded from output until inserted. Select a Source and use Make Selected Source Primary when Windows returns the files in an unexpected order. The former Primary moves to Source Media.

    Import conversion: Video and audio are converted locally to a consistent app-owned working format before editing. The original file is preserved. Large files may take time; wait for the loaded-duration announcement.

    Dedicated media editors: Every playable Primary and Source item has its own titled editor section, preview player, playhead slider, position, marks, and controls.

    Activating media: Arrow to an item in Primary Sequence or Source Media to activate it, or press Enter to move directly into its editor. Opening another source does not take control away from the Primary editor.

    Playback: X plays or pauses continuously. With brackets set, Space auditions the complete selection. Without brackets, Space plays continuously from the playhead. Press Space again to stop and return to the original position.

    Scrubbing: U and I scrub by one second. Shift+U and Shift+I scrub by 100 milliseconds. Ctrl+Alt+U and Ctrl+Alt+I scrub by 10 milliseconds. Alt+Left Arrow and Alt+Right Arrow move by 1 millisecond. Scrubbing intentionally produces no JAWS status announcement so you can hear the media sample.

    Quiet interaction: List choices and routine transport announcements are intentionally brief. Moving between sections does not add a second announcement after JAWS identifies the region. Reaching the end announces once. Rapid scrubbing does not announce expected interrupted-playback errors.

    Track targeting: Track targets are chosen only in Primary Production. B replaces both audio and video, V replaces video while preserving existing audio, and A replaces audio while preserving existing video. Source editors only select and copy media.

    Setting edit points: Left bracket sets the left boundary. Right bracket sets the right boundary. Ctrl+G opens exact-time fields.

    Canceling marks: Ctrl+Z removes the most recently set uncommitted mark. Escape cancels all uncommitted marks in the active editor.

    Replacing a track: In Primary Production, choose A, V, or B and mark the non-empty destination range with Left and Right Brackets. In a Source editor, mark a matching selection and press Ctrl+C. Return to Primary Production and press Ctrl+V. The Primary target controls the replacement; no target choice is required in the Source.

    Inserting into the sequence: With no Primary selection, Ctrl+V inserts at the playhead. Empty brackets also create an insertion point. Insertion preserves all original media and increases production duration. Ctrl+Shift+V remains an explicit Insert command but is not required for ordinary insertion.

    Using bracketed selections: A lone Left Bracket selects everything to its right. A lone Right Bracket selects everything to its left. Outward-facing brackets, Left then Right on the timeline, select the content between them. Inward-facing brackets, Right then Left on the timeline, select the beginning and end while leaving the middle unselected. Either bracket may be placed first. The command determines what happens: Ctrl+C copies the selected Source content, while Ctrl+Delete deletes the selected content.

    Editing a Source: The same brackets and Ctrl+Delete edit the active Source working timeline. Source playback and Ctrl+C use the edited timeline. The original Source file remains unchanged.

    Immediate review: After Ctrl+Delete, playback, the playhead, Home and End, and U and I scrubbing use the edited timeline immediately. You do not need to export before hearing the result.

    Returning to reading: Press Insert+Z to turn the Virtual Cursor on again.

    Native Open and Save dialogs: Virtual Cursor settings do not apply inside Windows file dialogs.

    Saving the complete production: Press Ctrl+E, choose the output format, and continue. FFmpeg renders every video and audio item in Primary Production in timeline order, then opens the Windows Save dialog. Original files remain unchanged.
