Accessible Media Editor Build 0.11.40

Opening and closing effect timing

Opening and closing effects now add time to the presentation instead of consuming time from the first and final items.

An opening effect prepends its duration. The first visual frame fades in from the selected color. Attached dialogue, foreground audio, and background music begin after the opening completes.

A closing effect appends its duration. The final visual frame remains visible while it fades to the selected color. Background music continues through the closing and fades to silence. Foreground audio ends with the final presentation item.

Automatic music-ducking ranges are shifted by the added opening duration so they remain synchronized with foreground audio.

The presentation summary includes the added opening and closing durations.
