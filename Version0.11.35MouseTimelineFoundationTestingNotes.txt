Accessible Media Editor Build 0.11.35

This build begins the shared mouse, keyboard, and screen-reader Presentation experience.

The existing Presentation Timeline list remains the authoritative accessible editing interface.

A synchronized visual timeline now appears beneath it for mouse users. Each visual card shows its playback position, item name, media type, duration, and incoming transition. The current card has a stronger visible border.

Mouse controls on each card provide Select, Earlier, Later, Edit, and Remove. They operate on the same project items and use the same editing functions as the accessible timeline controls.

The visual cards are intentionally removed from the accessibility tree so screen-reader users do not encounter a duplicated timeline or duplicated controls. Every card action already has an accessible equivalent in the Presentation Timeline and its adjacent controls.

AudioStudio Pro is not activated prematurely in this build. The roadmap now requires shared project state and synchronized mouse, keyboard, and screen-reader representations before audio handoff controls are introduced.

Test with a mouse by selecting several visual cards, moving one earlier and later, opening its properties, and removing an expendable item. Confirm that the accessible Presentation Timeline reflects every change.

Test with JAWS and NVDA and confirm that the visual cards create no additional reading-order stops or repeated announcements.
