Build 0.12.13 Audio Editor Section Names Testing Notes

This build corrects repeated and ambiguous Audio Editor headings and region names.

The workspace tab remains Audio Editor.

The first section is Recording Studio. It contains microphone selection, recording profiles, and recording controls.

The second persistent section is Shared Audio Library. It contains Audiobooks, Music, Recordings, Other Audio, All Audio, import controls, sources, classification, exporting, removal, and pause shortening.

The final section is Audio Clip Editor. It contains playback, speed, playhead, brackets, exact time entry, and saving a bracketed range.

Review Recording appears between Recording Studio and Shared Audio Library only when a new recording is waiting for review.

Testing

Switch to Audio Editor and confirm that the tab is announced as Audio Editor and focus enters Recording Studio.

Use Ctrl+Page Down repeatedly. Confirm that the application announces Recording Studio, Review Recording when present, Shared Audio Library, and Audio Clip Editor instead of repeatedly saying Audio Editor section.

Use Ctrl+Page Up to move through the same sections in reverse order.

Navigate by headings and regions with the Virtual PC Cursor on. Confirm that each section has a unique name and that there are no duplicate Audio Editor section headings.

Turn the Virtual PC Cursor off or use NVDA Focus Mode. Confirm that normal controls, lists, transport shortcuts, and editing commands continue to work.

Press F1 and confirm that the Audio Editor instructions use the same three section names.

Confirm that existing recordings, audiobooks, shared audio, projects, and editing behavior are unchanged.
