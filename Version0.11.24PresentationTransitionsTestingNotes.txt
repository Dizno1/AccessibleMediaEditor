Accessible Media Editor Build 0.11.24

This build preserves the presentation properties, preview, background music, automatic ducking, project saving, and timeline editing verified in Build 0.11.23.

New presentation transition workflow

Move to the Presentation Timeline and select the item that the transition should lead into.

Press Alt+Enter or activate Presentation Item Properties.

Under Transition into this item, choose Crossfade.

Enter the transition duration in seconds. Millisecond values are supported. For example, 1.250 creates a one second, 250 millisecond crossfade.

Save Presentation Item Properties.

The timeline item announces the crossfade and its duration. The total presentation duration is reduced by the overlap duration.

The first timeline item cannot have an incoming transition.

Choose None to restore a direct cut into the selected item.

Recommended test

Open a saved presentation project.

Select the second timeline item.

Press Alt+Enter.

Choose Crossfade and enter 1.000 seconds.

Save the properties.

Confirm that focus returns to the same timeline item and that JAWS announces Crossfade 1 second.

Preview the complete presentation. Confirm that the first item dissolves into the second over one second and that their audio also crossfades when both items contain audio.

Export the presentation and confirm that the exported MP4 matches the preview.

Save the project, close it, reopen it, and confirm that the crossfade remains assigned to the same item.

Also confirm that background music continues playing and continues to duck during foreground video or audio.
