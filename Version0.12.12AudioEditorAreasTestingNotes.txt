Accessible Production Studio
Build 0.12.12 Audio Editor Areas Testing Notes

This build renames the AudioStudio Pro workspace to Audio Editor. It does not rename or replace the planned standalone AccessibleAudioStudioPro application.

What to test

Open Audio Editor and confirm that the workspace name is announced as Audio Editor.

Move through the five area buttons: Audiobooks, Music, Recordings, Other Audio, and All Audio. Each is an ordinary keyboard-operable button that announces whether it is pressed. Activating one filters the Shared Audio Library and moves focus into that list.

Confirm that Audiobooks contains imported book collections in natural chapter order. Original and shortened chapter collections should remain separate.

Confirm that Recordings contains microphone recordings created in the application.

In All Audio or Other Audio, select one or more non-audiobook sources and activate Classify Selected as Music. Confirm that the selected sources appear in Music. Then test Classify Selected as Other Audio.

Classification changes library metadata only. It must not move, duplicate, rename, edit, or delete the original audio file.

Audiobook chapters cannot be reclassified as Music or Other Audio. They remain associated with their book.

While Music is active, use Ctrl+O to import ordinary audio and confirm it appears under Music. In other areas, Ctrl+O imports ordinary audio under Other Audio. Opening an audiobook folder switches to Audiobooks.

Press F1 from each workspace and confirm that the instructions call the third workspace Audio Editor, explain all five areas, and identify AccessibleAudioStudioPro as a planned separate audio-focused application.

Regression checks

Confirm that Alt+S still reaches Shared Audio Library from Audio Editor.

Confirm that Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab still switch among Media Editor, Presentation, and Audio Editor.

Confirm that Ctrl+Shift+O still offers Open Project first and Open Audiobook or Audio Folder second from every workspace.

Confirm that saved projects, imported books, recordings, and existing shared-library sources remain available after upgrading.

Confirm that Virtual PC Cursor off or NVDA Focus Mode operation remains predictable throughout the five audio areas.
