Accessible Media Editor
Build 0.12.3 Testing Notes
November 12, 2026

Playback speed

Open audio in AudioStudio Pro Audio Editor or open media in Media Editor.
Choose a Playback speed from 0.75 through 2 times.
Confirm that playback changes speed without changing pitch.
Confirm that the selected speed is shared between AudioStudio Pro and Media Editor.
Confirm that playback speed does not change saved or exported duration.

Shorten long pauses

Open an audio source in AudioStudio Pro Audio Editor.
The default settings detect quiet pauses lasting at least 1.5 seconds and retain 0.5 seconds from each pause.
Activate Analyze and Create Shortened Copy.
Confirm that analysis begins immediately and reports how many pauses were found, how much time will be removed, and the proposed new duration.
Cancel once and confirm that no new source is created.
Run the operation again and confirm creation.
Confirm that the new source begins with Shortened Pauses followed by the original descriptive name.
Preview the new source and compare it with the original.
Confirm that the original remains available and unchanged.
Test long pauses at the beginning, middle, and end of a source.

Safety

Silence shortening creates a new rendered audio source only after confirmation.
It does not alter or delete the original source.
