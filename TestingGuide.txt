ACCESSIBLE MEDIA EDITOR BUILD 0.5.1 TESTING GUIDE

FIRST IMPORT REGRESSION TEST

1. Start with no media open.
2. Press Ctrl+O and choose one Primary video.
3. Confirm Loading selected media. Please wait is announced.
4. Confirm the Primary video remains Primary and does not become Source media.
5. Confirm no TypeError is announced.
6. If an import fails, confirm the entire import is rolled back and the previous editing session remains intact.

PRIMARY TRIM TEST

1. Open one video as Primary Media.
2. Turn the JAWS Virtual Cursor off with Insert+Z.
3. Enter the Primary editor.
4. Move to the desired beginning and press left bracket.
5. Confirm Trim start is announced.
6. Move to the desired ending and press right bracket.
7. Confirm Trim end is announced.
8. Activate Apply Primary Trim or press Ctrl+Delete.
9. Confirm the application announces the trim start, trim end, and resulting duration.
10. Press Home and X. Confirm playback begins at the trim start and stops once at the trim end.

SOURCE SELECTION AND INSERTION TEST

1. Open a second supported video as Source Media.
2. Enter its Source editor.
3. Move to the desired selection beginning and press left bracket.
4. Confirm Selection start is announced.
5. Move to the desired selection ending and press right bracket.
6. Confirm Selection end is announced.
7. Activate Insert Selection at Primary Playhead inside the same Source editor, or press Ctrl+Enter.
8. Confirm the selection appears in Primary Sequence.
9. Repeat with Ctrl+Shift+Enter and confirm the selection is added to the end.
10. Confirm the original Source file remains unchanged.

QUIET INTERACTION TEST

1. Arrow through Primary Sequence and Source Media.
2. Confirm JAWS reads only the short list option and does not append the detailed duration paragraph.
3. Tab within an editor.
4. Confirm there is one custom Play or Pause button and one custom playhead slider. The native video buttons and second scrubber should not appear.
5. Use X, Space, U, I, Home, End, and movement keys.
6. Confirm filenames are not repeated in routine transport announcements.
7. Press U or I rapidly and confirm no AbortError is announced.

UNSUPPORTED FORMAT TEST

1. Open a WMV file.
2. Confirm the application announces that WMV preview is not supported yet and conversion is required.
3. Enter that Source editor.
4. Confirm its unusable player, audition, and playhead controls are disabled.

KNOWN LIMITS

Automatic format conversion is not included yet. WMV requires the planned FFmpeg conversion engine.

The application does not yet render or export the complete Primary Sequence.

AudioStudio Pro recording and processing are not implemented in this build.
