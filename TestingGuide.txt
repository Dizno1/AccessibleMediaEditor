ACCESSIBLE MEDIA EDITOR BUILD 0.8.1 TESTING GUIDE

FIRST IMPORT REGRESSION TEST

1. Start with no media open.
2. Press Ctrl+O and choose one Primary video.
3. Confirm Loading selected media. Please wait is announced.
4. Confirm the Primary video remains Primary and does not become Source media.
5. Confirm no TypeError is announced.
6. If an import fails, confirm the entire import is rolled back and the previous editing session remains intact.

PRIMARY REAL EDIT TEST

1. Open one video as Primary Media.
2. Turn the JAWS Virtual Cursor off with Insert+Z.
3. Enter the Primary editor.
4. Move to the beginning of a short unwanted section and press left bracket.
5. Move to the end of the unwanted section and press right bracket.
6. Press Ctrl+Delete.
7. Confirm Edit applied and the shorter duration are announced.
8. Press Home and then X. Confirm playback skips the removed section immediately without export.
9. Use U, I, Left Arrow, Right Arrow, Home, End, and the playhead. Confirm all use the shorter edited timeline.
10. Press Ctrl+Z. Confirm the removed media is restored.
11. Repeat the edit and wait for Edited working file ready.
12. Press Ctrl+E and save the edited file.
13. Open the saved file outside the editor and confirm the section is physically absent.
14. Confirm the original file is unchanged.

SINGLE BRACKET LOGIC TEST

1. Open one video as Primary Media.
2. Move to the desired new ending and press left bracket only.
3. Press Ctrl+Delete.
4. Confirm everything to the right of the Left Bracket is deleted and the content to its left remains.
5. Press Ctrl+Z and confirm the ending returns.
6. Move to the desired new beginning and press right bracket only.
7. Press Ctrl+Delete.
8. Confirm everything to the left of the Right Bracket is deleted and the content to its right remains.
9. Press Ctrl+Z and confirm the beginning returns.
10. Set the Left Bracket at an earlier time and the Right Bracket at a later time.
11. Confirm Inward brackets and the selected content are announced without calling it deletable data.
12. Press Ctrl+Delete and confirm only the content between the inward-facing brackets is deleted.
13. Press Ctrl+Z and confirm the content returns.
14. Move to a desired clip beginning and set the Right Bracket.
15. Continue forward and set the Left Bracket at the desired clip ending.
16. Confirm Outward brackets announce that the beginning and end are selected and the middle is not selected.
17. Press Ctrl+Delete and confirm the selected beginning and end are deleted, leaving the unselected middle.
18. Press Ctrl+Z once and confirm both deleted ends return.
19. Press Ctrl+Y once and confirm both ends are deleted again.

CHANGE PRIMARY PRODUCTION TEST

1. Import several files at once.
2. In Source Media, select the file that should be Primary.
3. Activate Make Selected Source Primary.
4. Confirm the selected filename is announced as Primary Production.
5. Confirm the former Primary is announced as moved to Source Media.
6. Confirm the new Primary plays in the unified Primary Production editor.
7. Confirm the former Primary has its own Source editor and retains its edits.
8. Press Ctrl+Z once and confirm the original Primary and Source roles return.
9. Press Ctrl+Y once and confirm the swap is reapplied.
10. Repeat the command through Project, Make Selected Source Primary.

SOURCE COPY AND PRIMARY PASTE TEST

1. Open a second supported video as Source Media.
2. Enter its Source editor.
3. Move to the desired selection beginning and press left bracket.
4. Confirm Left Bracket and the selected area are announced.
5. Move to the desired selection ending and press right bracket.
6. Confirm Right Bracket and the enclosed selection are announced.
7. Press Ctrl+C and confirm the filename, copied duration, and cleared brackets are announced.
8. Set the Source brackets again, use Edit, Copy Source Selection, and confirm it performs the same command.
9. Create an outward selection by setting Right Bracket earlier and Left Bracket later. Press Ctrl+C and paste into Primary Production. Confirm both selected ends are copied in their original order and the unselected middle is not copied.
10. Press Ctrl+Page Up until Primary Production is focused.
11. Move its playhead to the desired insertion point and press Ctrl+V.
12. Confirm the pasted duration and insertion time are announced.
13. Move to another Primary position, use Edit, Paste into Primary Production, and confirm it performs the same command.
14. Press Home and X. Confirm Primary Production plays continuously through the original and pasted media.
14. Confirm no separate Inserted Video editor region was created.
15. Press Ctrl+Z and confirm the pasted material is removed from Primary Production.
16. Confirm the complete original Source file remains available and unchanged.

SOURCE REMOVAL TEST

1. Enter a Source editor and activate Remove Source from Project.
2. Confirm Source removed. Press Ctrl+Z to restore it. Original file unchanged is announced.
3. Confirm focus moves to the next Source editor, or to Primary Production if no Source remains.
4. Press Ctrl+Z once and confirm Source restored is announced, the Source editor returns, and focus returns to it.

SOURCE NONDESTRUCTIVE EDIT TEST

1. Enter a Source Video or Source Audio editor.
2. Set a lone Right Bracket and press Ctrl+Delete.
3. Confirm the beginning is deleted from immediate Source playback.
4. Confirm Activate Primary Production first is not announced.
5. Press Home and X and confirm playback begins at the edited Source beginning.
6. Set brackets around material in the edited Source and press Ctrl+C.
7. Paste into Primary Production and confirm the copied material comes from the edited Source timeline.
8. Confirm the original Source file remains unchanged.

EDITED SOURCE PLAYHEAD ACCURACY TEST

1. Delete several seconds from the beginning of a Source.
2. Press Home and X to begin playback of the edited Source.
3. When you hear a recognizable word, press X to pause.
4. Press Space and confirm the audition contains that same word or the immediately surrounding audio.
5. Press X twice several times and confirm each pause remains at the point actually heard rather than jumping forward.
6. Press Space and immediately press a bracket before the sample finishes. Confirm the bracket uses the original audition point rather than a later moving playback position.
7. Apply an edit with Ctrl+Delete, press Ctrl+Z once, and confirm the deleted material returns.
8. Press Ctrl+Y once and confirm the edit is reapplied.

QUIET INTERACTION TEST

1. Arrow through Primary Sequence and Source Media.
2. Confirm JAWS reads only the short list option and does not append the detailed duration paragraph.
3. Tab within an editor.
4. Confirm there is one custom Play or Pause button and one custom playhead slider. The native video buttons and second scrubber should not appear.
5. Use X, Space, U, I, Home, End, and movement keys.
6. Confirm filenames are not repeated in routine transport announcements.
7. Press U or I rapidly and confirm no AbortError is announced.
8. Press Space, U, and I and confirm JAWS does not say Auditioning or Returned over the media sample.

FORMAT CONVERSION TEST

1. Open a WMV file.
2. Confirm the application announces that it is converting the media and asks you to wait.
3. Confirm Media ready or the normal loaded-duration message is announced after conversion.
4. Enter the editor and confirm the WMV content now plays from its normalized working copy.
5. Confirm the original WMV file remains unchanged.

SOURCE MARK AND NAVIGATION TEST

1. Open a second file as Source Media and enter its editor.
2. Move several seconds forward and press Left Bracket.
3. Press Home and confirm the playhead returns to 0 rather than the Selection Start.
4. Press End and confirm the playhead moves to the full Source duration.
5. Set another mark and press Ctrl+Z. Confirm the most recent uncommitted mark is removed.
6. Set one or two marks and press Escape. Confirm all uncommitted marks are canceled.

KNOWN LIMITS

Every imported video or audio file is converted to an app-owned working format before editing. Large files may require significant conversion time. Progress percentages are not included yet.

Build 0.8.1 immediately plays the complete multi-item Primary Production. It creates and saves an edited working file for one Primary video or audio file, but it does not yet FFmpeg-render a multi-item production for export.

AudioStudio Pro recording and processing are not implemented in this build.
