ACCESSIBLE MEDIA EDITOR BUILD 0.4.4 TESTING GUIDE

PURPOSE

Build 0.4.4 stops expected AbortError speech during rapid scrubbing and removes filenames from all routine transport feedback. It retains the quiet-interaction corrections and separate editor for every playable Primary and Source item.

QUIET INTERACTION TEST

1. Arrow through Primary Sequence and Source Media.
2. Confirm each option speaks a short role, media type, and filename instead of duration, In, and Out details.
3. Move through editor sections with Ctrl+Page Up and Ctrl+Page Down.
4. Confirm JAWS identifies each region only once without a second application announcement.
5. Press X to play and pause. Confirm the application says only Playing or Paused followed by the time.
6. Set an Out mark, play toward it, and confirm Out mark reached is announced only once.
7. Press U or I rapidly and confirm no AbortError is announced and each new command replaces the previous scrub sample cleanly.
8. Use Space, Home, End, and the movement keys. Confirm their feedback does not repeat the active filename.

PRIMARY AND SOURCE TEST

1. Start Accessible Media Editor.
2. Turn the JAWS Virtual Cursor off with Insert+Z.
3. Press Ctrl+O and open one video.
4. Confirm that it appears in Primary Sequence.
5. Press Ctrl+O and open a second video.
6. Confirm that it appears in Source Media and does not increase Primary Sequence duration.
7. Confirm that importing the Source did not take keyboard control away from Primary Media.

DEDICATED EDITOR TEST

1. From Primary Sequence, arrow to the Primary video.
2. Press Enter. Confirm focus moves to a section named Primary Video followed by its title.
3. Tab through its preview player, playhead slider, position, transport, marks, exact-time, properties, split, and track-target controls.
4. Press Ctrl+Page Down until you reach the Source video editor.
5. Confirm its heading names the Source video and that it has a different preview player and slider.
6. Press Ctrl+Page Up and Ctrl+Page Down to cycle among Media Library, every media editor, and Output.

ACTIVE MEDIA AND PLAYBACK TEST

1. In Primary Sequence, arrow to Primary Media and press X. Confirm Primary plays.
2. Press X again to pause.
3. In Source Media, arrow to a Source and press X. Confirm that Source, not Primary, plays.
4. Press Space and confirm a short audition occurs around the active playhead and returns to it.
5. Confirm that each editor remembers its own playhead when you move between editors.

SCRUB, MARK, AND TARGET TEST

1. Press U and I to scrub backward and forward by one second.
2. Press Shift+U and Shift+I to scrub by 100 milliseconds.
3. Press Ctrl+Shift+U and Ctrl+Shift+I to scrub by 10 milliseconds.
4. Confirm that JAWS does not speak a filename, timestamp, or audition-complete message during any U/I scrub. The short media sample should remain audible.
5. Press left bracket to set the In mark and right bracket to set the Out mark.
6. Press B, V, and A in a video editor and confirm Both, Video only, and Audio only announcements.
7. Press Ctrl+G and enter Hours, Minutes, Seconds, and Milliseconds for an exact playhead position.

INSERT SOURCE SELECTION TEST

1. Activate a Source editor and mark a short selection.
2. Activate the Primary editor and put its playhead at the desired insertion point.
3. Return to Source Media without changing the Primary playhead.
4. Activate Insert Selection at Primary Playhead.
5. Confirm that the source selection appears in Primary Sequence and the original source file remains unchanged.

WORKSPACE TEST

1. Press Ctrl+Tab to enter AudioStudio Pro.
2. Confirm that it is an explicit placeholder and was not opened merely because an audio file was loaded.
3. Press Ctrl+Shift+Tab to return to Media Editor.

JAWS NOTES

- Keep the Virtual Cursor off while operating lists, sliders, and editing shortcuts.
- Turn it on with Insert+Z when you want to read the page conventionally.
- Virtual Cursor settings do not apply inside native Windows Open and Save dialogs.

KNOWN LIMITS

The application does not yet render or export the complete Primary Sequence.

AudioStudio Pro recording and processing are not implemented in this build.

Video-only targeting currently mutes audio during preview and records the intended edit target. Full track rendering comes with the composition engine.
