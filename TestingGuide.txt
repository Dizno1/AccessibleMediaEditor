ACCESSIBLE MEDIA EDITOR BUILD 0.4.0 TESTING GUIDE

PURPOSE

Build 0.4.0 separates Primary Media from Source Media and introduces the optional AudioStudio Pro workspace.

PRIMARY AND SOURCE TEST

1. Start Accessible Media Editor.
2. Press Ctrl+O and open one video.
3. Confirm that it appears in Primary Sequence.
4. Press Ctrl+O and open a second video.
5. Confirm that the second video appears in Source Media.
6. Confirm that the primary sequence duration does not include the second video's complete duration.

ACTIVE MEDIA TEST

1. Select the Primary Sequence item.
2. Move to Playback with Ctrl+Page Down.
3. Confirm that the Playback heading includes the primary title.
4. Return to Media and select the Source Media item.
5. Confirm that Playback and Edit headings now include the source title.
6. Confirm that the application announces whether Primary Sequence or Source Media is active.

TRANSPORT TEST

1. Press X to start playback from the active playhead.
2. Press X again to pause and land at the new position.
3. Press Space.
4. Confirm that a short section around the playhead is auditioned.
5. Confirm that the playhead returns to its original position after auditioning.
6. Press Escape during an audition and confirm that the audition is canceled. Escape should not be the ordinary playback control.

SCRUB AND MARK TEST

1. Press U and I to scrub backward and forward by one second.
2. Press Shift+U and Shift+I to scrub by 100 milliseconds.
3. Press Ctrl+Shift+U and Ctrl+Shift+I to scrub by 10 milliseconds.
4. Press left bracket to set the In mark.
5. Press right bracket to set the Out mark.

TRACK TARGET TEST

1. With video active, press B and confirm Both audio and video targeted.
2. Press V and confirm Video only targeted.
3. Press A and confirm Audio only targeted.
4. Open an audio file and confirm that it begins in Audio only mode.

INSERT SOURCE SELECTION TEST

1. Activate the Source Media item.
2. Use the playhead and brackets to mark a short selection.
3. Return to Primary Sequence and place its playhead at the desired insertion point.
4. Return to Source Media without changing the Primary playhead.
5. Activate Insert Selection at Primary Playhead.
6. Confirm that the source selection appears in Primary Sequence.
7. Confirm that the original source file remains unchanged.

WORKSPACE TEST

1. Press Ctrl+Tab to enter AudioStudio Pro.
2. Confirm that its professional controls are disabled and clearly described as future work.
3. Press Ctrl+Shift+Tab to return to Media Editor.
4. Confirm that the active media and playhead position were preserved.

KNOWN LIMITS

The application does not yet render or export the complete Primary Sequence.

AudioStudio Pro recording and processing are not implemented in this build.

Video-only targeting identifies the intended edit target and mutes audio during preview. Full track rendering comes with the composition engine.
