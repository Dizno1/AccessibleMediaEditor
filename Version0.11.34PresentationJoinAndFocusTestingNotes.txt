Accessible Media Editor Build 0.11.34 Testing Notes

This build addresses the presentation-preview failure that occurred after all 11 individual items rendered successfully.

Before joining items, the renderer now normalizes every intermediate video to 1280 by 720, 30 frames per second, YUV 4:2:0 pixel format, a common time base, and timestamps starting at zero.

Every intermediate audio stream is normalized to 48 kilohertz stereo floating-point audio with timestamps starting at zero.

Transitions, opening effects, and closing effects are applied only after that normalization.

If joining still fails, StartupLog now identifies the joining stage and preserves a longer section of the FFmpeg diagnostic output.

Focus correction

After Apply Opening and Closing Effects is activated, focus returns to the first item in the Presentation Timeline. In the current test project, that is Title Slide - Dogs At Play.

Saving Presentation Item Properties continues to return focus to the presentation item whose properties were saved.

Open DogsFirst Test Presentation.

Apply Fade in from black and Fade out to white.
