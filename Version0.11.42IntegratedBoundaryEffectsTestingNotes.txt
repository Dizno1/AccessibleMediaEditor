Accessible Media Editor
Build 0.11.42 Testing Notes

This build changes opening and closing effects so the first and final presentation items count toward the requested effect durations.

Example using the current Dogs presentation

The opening effect is 10 seconds.
The title slide and its attached audio are 4 seconds, 702 milliseconds.
The renderer adds only 5 seconds, 298 milliseconds before the title slide.
The title slide and its audio then fade in during the remaining 4 seconds, 702 milliseconds of the opening.
Background music remains silent until the complete 10-second opening has finished.

The same rule applies at the end. The final presentation item counts toward the requested closing duration. Only the additional time needed to complete the closing effect is appended.

The final presentation audio mix fades out with the closing effect. This includes background music and foreground sound.

Recommended test

Open DogsFirst Test Presentation.ameproject.
Confirm that the title slide still has its attached audio and duration matching.
Set the opening effect to Fade in from black with a duration of 10 seconds.
Set the closing effect to Fade out to white with a duration of 10 seconds.
Preview the complete presentation.

Expected opening result

The preview begins in silence.
The title audio begins about 5 seconds, 298 milliseconds after the preview starts.
The title audio fades in rather than beginning at full volume.
The title slide completes at the 10-second point.
Background music begins at the 10-second point.

Expected closing result

The visual closing effect includes the final item instead of starting after it.
The final mixed audio fades continuously throughout the closing effect.
Only the extra time needed beyond the final item duration is added.
